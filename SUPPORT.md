# Support

- **Something does not work, or a refusal reads wrong:** open an issue at
  <https://github.com/TensusDS/flotilla/issues>. Include the output of `/flotilla:doctor` and the exact text flotilla
  printed - its refusals say what they checked.
- **A security concern:** do not open a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **How it works and how to fix common problems:** [README.md](README.md) and the
  [Troubleshooting section of USER_MANUAL.md](USER_MANUAL.md#troubleshooting).

flotilla is maintained by TensusDS. Issues are read and answered; a confirmed defect is fixed with a test that fails
first, and released with a new version.
